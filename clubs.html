Financial Benefits

Your club earns.
Every month.

One of the most compelling reasons to partner with Gymnastics Online is simple: your club earns money every time a family joins using your club code. No extra work. No selling. Just ongoing passive income.

1

First 3 months — 25% of every membership

For each family that signs up using your club code, your club earns 25% of their membership fee for the first three months.

2

Ongoing — 10% forever

After the first 3 months, your club earns 10% of every membership fee, every month, for as long as that family stays a member.

3

Zero effort required

Families simply sign up using your club's unique code. No lock-in, no selling, no admin. Every family that joins adds to your club's income.

💡 Example: A club with 80 families, 50 of whom join GO. Your club immediately earns 25% of their monthly memberships for 3 months, then 10% every month after — indefinitely. Completely passive.

Platform Features

Everything your club needs.
Built for how coaches actually work.

Beyond the financial incentive, GO gives your club a powerful set of tools that improve athlete outcomes, streamline coach workload, and elevate your club's professionalism.

01

Home Conditioning & Workout Modules

Coaches can assign specific conditioning and flexibility modules directly to individual athletes for home practice. With a library of 250+ exercises — and growing — coaches can build fully custom workouts.

  • 250+ expert-approved exercises at launch
  • New exercises added continuously
  • Assign to individual athletes or whole squads
  • Purpose-built for gymnastics — not adapted from generic fitness
02

Reporting & Communication Tool

The feature coaches love most. Record a voice note from your phone — the platform converts it into a beautifully presented, professional report delivered directly to the family. No typing required.

  • Voice-to-report in under 60 seconds
  • Question library from Baby Gym to International pathways
  • Ad hoc, injury, and end-of-term reports
  • Coach privacy protected — no personal contact details shared
  • Club controls the approval workflow
03

Athlete Wellness Check-In

Rooted in the athlete-parent-coach triangle, the Wellness Check-In ensures every athlete is seen as a whole person. Before training, athletes answer a brief check-in covering physical and psychological readiness.

  • Physical and mental wellbeing check before each session
  • Red flag responses surfaced immediately to the coach
  • Best-practice coach response prompts included
  • Completable on phone or club iPad before training

Common Questions

We've heard your concerns.

Here are the questions we hear most from clubs — and our honest answers.

"We don't want more back-and-forth with parents."
Two-way communication is entirely optional and can be switched off. Coaches control their working hours — messages from parents only arrive during the window coaches set.
"The wellness check-ins sound like extra work."
They're optional and can be disabled at any time. For clubs that do use them, they take under a minute and have been credited with surfacing issues coaches would never have known about.
"We already do injury reporting."
This just makes what you're already doing faster, cleaner, and stored automatically. Voice-to-report means the whole thing takes under 60 seconds.
"Will our coaches actually use it?"
It's designed for the phone and built for speed. The reporting tool was specifically created so coaches who hate admin will actually use it — because it takes less time than typing a text.
"We already have a system."
Fair. Our question is simple: does your current system pay you? Every family that joins GO with your club code earns your club passive income — month after month.
"We're a small club. Is this for us?"
Perfect for small clubs. Low effort, passive income, and tools that actually reduce coach workload. You don't need 200 families to make this worthwhile.
