Selective Partnership

Is this your club?

We’re selective about who we partner with — not because we’re exclusive, but because we’re building something together. The clubs we want to hear from share a few things in common.

You believe parent communication is part of athlete development

Not a distraction from it. You want parents informed, confident, and aligned — not in the dark or guessing.

You think about retention and why families leave

You understand that churn is costly — financially and culturally — and you’re actively looking for ways to keep the families you’ve worked hard to bring in.

You take athlete wellness seriously

You see your athletes as whole people — not just gymnasts. Physical readiness, mental wellbeing, and a positive relationship with the sport all matter to you.

You want to be known as a best-practice club

Reputation matters. You want your club to be the one other clubs look to — for professionalism, athlete outcomes, and the environment you create.

You’re open to doing things differently

You don’t assume the way things have always been done is the best way. If there’s a smarter approach that benefits your athletes and families, you want to know about it.

You want your club to benefit financially from what you recommend

When you point families toward something great, it’s fair that your club shares in that. We agree.

If you’re nodding along — we’d love to talk.
