How we work together

Ways to collaborate

There's no single way to contribute. Whether you want to shape the content, grow the community, or align your brand with GO — there's a place for you.

Content Collaborator

Contribute articles, guides, video content or expert commentary across any discipline — nutrition, mental performance, coaching, parenting, and more.

Technical Contributor

Help shape the platform itself — from exercise library design and workout programming to data analysis, research integration, and app functionality.

Affiliate Partner

Earn ongoing commissions by introducing GO to clubs, families and coaches in your network. Our affiliate programme rewards you for every member you bring on board.

Expert Advisor

Lend your professional expertise to validate our content and guide our direction. Be listed as a GO expert and reach thousands of gymnastics families worldwide.

Brand Partner

Align your brand with a trusted platform serving gymnastics families globally. Opportunities span merchandise, equipment, apparel, and nutrition categories.

Community Leader

Facilitate conversations, champion regions or disciplines, and help gymnastics families connect meaningfully within the GO community.

What you get

Why collaborate with GO?

We believe collaboration should genuinely benefit everyone involved. Here's what being part of the GO community means for you.

Early Access to Features

Be the first to use, test and give feedback on new features before they go live. Your expertise shapes the product directly.

Recognition & Visibility

Be formally listed as a GO expert, contributor, or partner. Build your professional profile and reach an audience you couldn't easily access on your own — so you can focus on what you do best.

Global Network Access

Connect with a diverse, like-minded community of gymnastics professionals across multiple disciplines and countries.

Financial Rewards

We offer affiliate commissions, content remuneration, and revenue-sharing arrangements — discussed openly and individually. We believe in rewarding contribution fairly and sustainably.

Brand Growth Support

We actively promote our collaborators. If marketing yourself isn't your strength — or your focus — we handle that so you can stay in your zone of genius: your athletes, your clients, your craft.

Founding Collaborator Status

Those who join early hold a special place in the GO story. Founding collaborators receive preferential terms and permanent recognition as part of the team that built this.

A note on remuneration: We don't make blanket promises about compensation — because every collaboration is different. What we can promise is an honest conversation about what's fair for your contribution, your time, and your expertise. We're building something long-term, and we want everyone involved to benefit as GO grows.

The process

How it works

Getting involved is straightforward. We move quickly and we're respectful of your time.

1

Reach Out

Fill out the expression of interest form. Tell us who you are, what you do, and how you'd like to contribute. No lengthy applications — just a genuine conversation starter.

2

Connect

We personally review every expression of interest. You'll hear from Kym directly within a few business days. If there's a fit, we'll set up a call to explore it properly.

3

Contract and Onboarding

We onboard you properly — no being left to figure it out alone. We will make a clear plan together. You will have access to GO resources and a direct line to the GO team from day one.
