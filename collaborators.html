How we work together

Ways to collaborate

There's no single way to contribute. Whether you want to shape the content, grow the community, or align your brand with GO — there's a place for you.

On or off camera

Technical Gymnastics Contributor

Help shape GO's training content from the inside. Design exercises, build programming, and contribute the technical gymnastics knowledge that sets us apart from generic fitness platforms — whether you're in front of the camera or behind it.

On or off camera

Allied Content Collaborator

Bring your specialist expertise to gymnastics families who need it. Contribute articles, guides, video content or resources across nutrition, mental performance, sports science, parenting, and more — on camera or in writing.

Affiliate Partner

Earn ongoing commissions for every member you bring to GO. Introduce us to clubs, families, and coaches in your network and get rewarded every month for as long as they stay. No selling — just sharing something you believe in.

Club Partner

Partner your club with GO and give your families access to the home training platform built for their gymnast. Clubs earn affiliate income from every family who subscribes and position themselves as forward-thinking environments serious about whole-athlete development.

Club partner details →

Merchandise Partner

Align your brand with a values-led platform serving gymnastics families globally. If you're in merchandise, equipment, apparel, or nutrition — let's explore how we can build something that genuinely benefits the community and your business.

Brand Ambassador

Be the face of GO in your region. We're looking for respected voices in the gymnastics world to front campaigns, champion the platform, and help build trust with athletes and families at scale. If that sounds like you, we'd love to hear from you.

Commercial Partner

Our long-term vision is a globally consistent platform with locally grounded leadership. Regional Commercial Partners take ownership of membership growth in their territory while ensuring GO's content remains relevant for their gymnastics community. If this resonates, let's have a conversation.

General Contributor

You don't need a specific brief to be part of this. If you believe in what we're building and you have time, skills, or connections to offer — reach out. There are more ways to contribute than we could ever list on a page.

What you get

Why collaborate with GO?

We believe collaboration should genuinely benefit everyone involved. Here's what being part of the GO community means for you.

Early Access to Features

Be the first to use, test and give feedback on new features before they go live. Your expertise shapes the product directly.

Recognition & Visibility

Be formally listed as a GO expert, contributor, or partner. Build your professional profile and reach an audience you couldn't easily access on your own — so you can focus on what you do best.

Global Network Access

Connect with a diverse, like-minded community of gymnastics professionals across multiple disciplines and countries.

Financial Rewards

We offer affiliate commissions, content remuneration, and revenue-sharing arrangements — discussed openly and individually. We believe in rewarding contribution fairly and sustainably.

Brand Growth Support

We actively promote our collaborators. If marketing yourself isn't your strength — or your focus — we handle that so you can stay in your zone of genius: your athletes, your clients, your craft.

Founding Collaborator Status

Those who join early hold a special place in the GO story. Founding collaborators receive preferential terms and permanent recognition as part of the team that built this.

A note on remuneration: The form compensation takes varies by contribution type and scope. Affiliates earn commissions on every referral. Content contributors receive upfront fees for work produced. Some collaborators provide time or resources in exchange for outbound referrals. We're transparent about compensation from the first conversation and work out an arrangement for each relationship that is mutually beneficial.

The process

How it works

Getting involved is straightforward. We move quickly and we're respectful of your time.

1

Reach Out

Fill out the expression of interest form. Tell us who you are, what you do, and how you'd like to contribute. No lengthy applications — just a genuine conversation starter.

2

Connect

We personally review every expression of interest. You'll hear from Kym directly within a few business days. If there's a fit, we'll set up a call to explore it properly.

3

Contract and Onboarding

We onboard you properly — no being left to figure it out alone. We will make a clear plan together. You will have access to GO resources and a direct line to the GO team from day one.
