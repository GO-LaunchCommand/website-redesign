How we work together

Ways to collaborate

There's no single way to contribute. Whether you want to shape the content, grow the community, or align your brand with GO — there's a place for you.

Content Collaborator

Contribute articles, guides, video content or expert commentary across any discipline — nutrition, mental performance, coaching, parenting, and more.

Technical Contributor

Help shape the platform itself — from exercise library design and workout programming to data analysis, research integration, and app functionality.

Affiliate Partner

Earn revenue by introducing GO to clubs, families and coaches in your network. Our affiliate programme rewards you for every member you bring on board.

Expert Advisor

Lend your professional expertise to validate our content and guide our direction. Be listed as a GO expert and reach thousands of gymnastics families.

Brand Partner

Align your brand with a trusted platform serving gymnastics families globally. Opportunities in merchandise, equipment, apparel, and nutrition categories.

Community Leader

Facilitate conversations, moderate community spaces, champion regions or disciplines, and help families connect meaningfully within the GO community.
